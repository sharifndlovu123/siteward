Usage policy & privacy

Acceptable use

This tool runs passive security checks. Only scan sites you own or are explicitly authorised to test. Scans are rate limited, and abuse may be reported to relevant parties.

What we store

  • Scan results are held in server memory only, for about {{ ttl_minutes }} minutes after a scan finishes, then discarded. They are never written to disk or a database.
  • To handle abuse reports, we keep a minimal log for {{ retention_days }} days: time, your IP address, a keyed hash of the scanned hostname, which options were used, and the outcome. We do not log findings, page content or full URLs.
  • Our network provider (Cloudflare) keeps its own request logs under its own policy.
